Executive readout

Spej has the right operating model. It lacks the mature plumbing.

The Control Center is already unusually strong at joining relationship selling, partnerships, content, market intelligence, project work, and human-approved AI. The largest gap is not another dashboard. It is automatic data capture, workflow execution, forecasting discipline, data governance, and multi-user controls.

Do not recreate Salesforce. Build a Spej-specific revenue operating layer, then connect specialized systems where they are genuinely better.

Spej should own the operating experience: what needs attention, the relationship and buying context, the next best action, content opportunities, delivery handoff, and Sosa. It should integrate rather than rebuild email delivery, contact databases, calling, e-signature, and meeting recording.

Current product maturity

6/10 as a personal operating system

Approximately 3/10 as a secure, shared company system because identity, permissions, concurrency, integration jobs, and auditability are not production-ready.

Response strategy

Recommended product sequence

Phase 1 · Make it trustworthy

  • Real user identity and roles
  • Normalized multi-user data model
  • Record-level change history
  • Outlook/Teams/calendar capture
  • Dedupe and merge
  • Safe agent permissions

Phase 2 · Make it proactive

  • Workflow rules and SLAs
  • Stage aging and deal health
  • Forecast and conversion math
  • Meeting-to-CRM extraction
  • Buying-committee coverage
  • Daily Sosa priorities

Phase 3 · Make it scalable

  • Campaign attribution
  • Reusable playbooks/templates
  • Goals and portfolios
  • Workload and dependency views
  • Proposals/approvals/handoffs
  • Teams/chat and mobile entry

What not to build

Buy or integrate these capabilities

  • B2B contact and intent database
  • Email delivery reputation infrastructure
  • Calling and call recording infrastructure
  • E-signature and payment processing
  • General-purpose document storage
  • A thousand-connector marketplace

Keep Spej opinionated

  • Relationship context before activity volume
  • Buying committees rather than isolated contacts
  • SPIN and disciplined prospecting as prompts, not rigid gates
  • Human approval for consequential Sosa updates
  • Sales, partnerships, delivery, and content connected but distinct
  • Aby-optimized Today view with role-specific team views later
The single highest-leverage next release: Microsoft activity capture + a review inbox. Let Sosa turn meetings, emails, and Teams context into proposed activities, contacts, opportunity changes, and tasks. This attacks manual data entry while preserving accuracy and human control.
